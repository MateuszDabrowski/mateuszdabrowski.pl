### AMPScript

AMPScript is a proprietary scripting language in Salesforce :product[Marketing Cloud Engagement] along with [SSJS](#server-side-javascript). Out of the two, it is simpler and more performant, but too limited for complex solutions. Good starting point for marketers without development experience. Best option for scripting in messaging.
