</details>

## Privacy Policy {#privacy-policy}

<p style={{fontStyle: 'italic', color: 'var(--ifm-color-emphasis-600)'}}>Last updated: September 4, 2026</p>

<p>Shelf is built by Mateusz Dąbrowski. This policy explains what the Shelf app does with your data. It is short because there is not much to say: Shelf sends nothing to me, and what it keeps, it encrypts on your device first.</p>

    <div style={{marginTop: '2rem'}}>

### Nothing reaches me

    </div>

<p>I operate no servers for Shelf. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no way for your data to reach me. Shelf contains <strong>no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs of any kind</strong> - it is built entirely against Apple's own frameworks. Its only network traffic is Apple's iCloud sync, described below.</p>

    <div style={{marginTop: '2rem'}}>

### What Shelf stores, and how

    </div>

<p>Everything you put into Shelf is saved on your device and, if you are signed into iCloud, in <strong>your private iCloud database</strong> through Apple's CloudKit. It is visible only to you, on devices signed into your Apple Account. I cannot see it.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>The names and values of your stuff</strong> are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before they are saved or synced. Only ciphertext is ever stored, on the device or in iCloud.</li>
  <li><strong>The encryption key</strong> is generated on your device and kept in your iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end. It is never sent anywhere else, and it is not readable by Apple or by me.</li>
  <li><strong>Folder names and icons, each item's type, whether it is hidden or pinned, how often you have used it, and your sort order</strong> are synced through iCloud with Apple's transport encryption but are not separately encrypted by Shelf. Folders need readable names to be navigated, and the type decides which action a row offers. These are visible to Apple as record metadata.</li>
  <li><strong>Device-local settings</strong> - appearance, the global shortcut, App Lock, whether widgets are enabled, per-device folder visibility - stay on the device that set them.</li>
</ul>

    <div style={{marginTop: '2rem'}}>

### Data Shelf does not collect

    </div>

<p>Shelf does <strong>not</strong> collect, transmit or store any of the following: personal information, account credentials, analytics or usage data, crash reports, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or your browsing. The App Privacy label on the App Store is "Data Not Collected", and this policy is why.</p>

    <div style={{marginTop: '2rem'}}>

### The clipboard

    </div>

<p>Copying to the clipboard is what Shelf is for: a value is written to the clipboard when you tap a row, and only then. Shelf reads text only from what you paste into its own add form, to guess the item's type; it has no clipboard observer and never reads the clipboard in the background. When you copy a value you marked hidden, Shelf tags it so clipboard managers skip it and clears it from the clipboard after 60 seconds.</p>

    <div style={{marginTop: '2rem'}}>

### App Lock

    </div>

<p>On iPhone and iPad you can require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open Shelf. The check is performed by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Shelf receives only a yes or no; it never sees, stores or transmits biometric data.</p>

    <div style={{marginTop: '2rem'}}>

### Widgets and the Share sheet

    </div>

<p>Home Screen widgets are off by default. If you turn them on, the items you pick are decrypted on your device to be drawn on the Home Screen; hidden values stay masked, and on a locked device iOS redacts the widget entirely. The Share extension saves the text you share into Shelf and nothing else - it reads no other content from the app you shared from.</p>

    <div style={{marginTop: '2rem'}}>

### iCloud

    </div>

<p>Sync uses your own iCloud account and Apple's infrastructure; Apple's privacy policy governs it: <a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/">apple.com/legal/privacy</a>. Without an iCloud account, or if you sign out, Shelf keeps working with the data on the device and stops syncing. You can turn iCloud off for Shelf alone in your device's iCloud settings.</p>

    <div style={{marginTop: '2rem'}}>

### Deleting your data

    </div>

<p>Delete an item or a folder in Shelf and it is removed from the device and from your iCloud database on every synced device. Deleting the app removes its local data; what is in iCloud can be removed under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage › Shelf. The encryption key in your iCloud Keychain can be removed from Shelf's Settings › Security, which makes any remaining ciphertext permanently unreadable.</p>
