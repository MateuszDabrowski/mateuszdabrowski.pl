Twelve years in MarTech, the last six in the Salesforce ecosystem. I started on the client side, in e-commerce and digital marketing for a legal publisher. There I taught myself Python to automate my own work, then led the move to Marketing Automation on Oracle Eloqua and built the tooling around it. What I learned there still drives my work: automation pays off only when marketing, sales, data and IT cooperate. The platform has to be built for that.

Global campaign operations on Adobe Campaign for a pharma company came next. In 2020 I switched to Salesforce: six years in a Big Four Marketing Cloud practice. Consultant, then architect, team lead, manager and acting practice lead of thirty-plus Salesforce and Adobe experts. A dozen engagements in those years. They ranged from custom Cloud Page apps and Agentforce proofs of concept to Marketing Cloud implementations for fifty-plus business units, in pharma, manufacturing, consumer goods, energy, education and travel.

Today I work as a principal architect on Salesforce MarTech, across Marketing Cloud, Data 360 and Agentforce implementations. AI agents are a growing part of every engagement, and Claude is part of my own daily workflow. I also build tools that make the work simpler for me and others, like [Diagramforce](https://diagramforce.com) for architecture diagrams or [Slot](/slot/) for managing multiple inboxes. And I share my knowledge with the community.
