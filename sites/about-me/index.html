Twelve years in MarTech, the last six in the Salesforce ecosystem. I started on the client side, in e-commerce and digital marketing for a legal publisher. There I taught myself Python to automate my own work, then led the move to Marketing Automation on Oracle Eloqua and built the tooling around it. What I learned there still drives my work: automation pays off only when marketing, sales, data and IT cooperate. The platform has to be built for that.

Global campaign operations on Adobe Campaign for a pharma company came next. In 2020 I switched to Salesforce: six years in a Big Four Marketing Cloud practice. Consultant, then architect, team lead, manager and acting practice lead of thirty-plus Salesforce and Adobe experts. A dozen engagements in those years. They ranged from custom Cloud Page apps and Agentforce proofs of concept to Marketing Cloud implementations for fifty-plus business units, in pharma, manufacturing, consumer goods, energy, education and travel.

Today I work as a principal architect on Salesforce MarTech, across Marketing Cloud, Data 360 and Agentforce implementations. AI agents are a growing part of every engagement, and Claude is part of my own daily workflow. I also build tools that make the work simpler for me and others, like Diagramforce for architecture diagrams or Slot for managing multiple inboxes. And I share my knowledge with the community.

What I do

Architecture

Contact, profile and consent data models. Multi-org and multi-business-unit setups. Integration with Sales, Service and Health Cloud through Marketing Cloud Connect. Web Studio architecture built for performance and security.

Technical leadership

Running the build team on implementations from scratch, including the deployers and naming conventions that make a fifty-business-unit rollout repeatable.

Audit and rescue

Taking over an existing Marketing Cloud or Personalization implementation, finding what is wrong, and refactoring it into something the client's own team can run.

AI agents

Practical use of AI and agents across Agentforce and Claude. Agentic workflows that take manual steps out of internal and external processes, and ways to get the most out of Salesforce systems with them.

Custom applications

Cloud Page apps, API integrations and internal tools on Marketing Cloud. On my own time: Diagramforce, Slot, Shelf and Strum.

Selected work

Two Marketing Cloud Engagement orgs for a global pharma company

Pharma and healthcarePwC · 2021 to 2022

The problem. Fifty-plus business units across countries, Marketing Cloud connected to Health Cloud, and consent that must update in every system as soon as it changes in one.

What I did. Led the Marketing Cloud build team. Designed the contact model and the multi-level consent model for both multi-org and single-org setups, wired through Marketing Cloud Connect. Built fifty-plus preference centres on a consent backend that pushes changes across clouds in real time. Added a privacy-first contact deletion and archiving process, and a custom deployer for the multi-country rollout. Left a naming convention and a refactoring plan behind.

What changed. One consent model for every business unit, kept in step with Health Cloud in real time, and a deployer that cut the time to roll out each country.

Marketing Cloud Engagement and Personalization for a manufacturer

ManufacturingPwC · 2023 to 2024

The problem. Several build streams, one client, and a golden customer record that lives in the CRM while consent gets collected in marketing channels.

What I did. Marketing Cloud architect across the streams. Owned the data model requirements and their dependencies. Designed the profile and consent flows through Marketing Cloud Connect to Sales and Service Cloud, and a closed-loop, real-time consent process that keeps the golden record in the CRM. Defined the performance- and security-focused Web Studio architecture and supported the build.

What changed. Consent captured in any channel lands on the CRM record in real time, and Web Studio runs on an architecture built for performance and security.

Interaction Studio audit and rebuild for a consumer electronics retailer

E-commercePwC · 2022 to 2023

The problem. An existing Interaction Studio and Marketing Cloud implementation due for an audit. New use cases were waiting on it, and the client's data team had no way to feed it new signals.

What I did. Audited both platforms. Refactored the sitemap, the web campaign templates and configuration, the Einstein recipes and the catalog. Extended the catalog with custom objects so the ML models had new features to work with. Built new use cases against the client's needs and worked with their data teams on the indicators driving real-time personalisation.

What changed. A platform the client's own data team can extend, with new indicators and catalog objects feeding the recipes.

Agentforce enablement for a global professional services network

Professional servicesPwC · 2024 to 2025

The problem. Agentforce had just launched. A global firm had to understand it, demo it, sell it and build it, with no playbook to follow.

What I did. Led the discovery, with a focus on knowledge-based agents. Built the Agentforce World Tour demos for teams around the world, a cross-alliance Agentforce and Workday proof of concept, and the guide covering up-skilling, pre-sales, build and implementation. Designed and ran a three-day global hackathon on understanding, designing, building and selling Agentforce.

What changed. Contracts won for some of the first Agentforce implementations in the world, sold with use-case-driven demos.
