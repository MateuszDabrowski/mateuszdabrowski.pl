## How the Newsletter Works

I send it with Sling, my own newsletter app, built around your privacy and email best practices:

- **No tracking.** I don't track whether you open an email or click anything. In fact, I built Sling so it can't: every link and image in an email is the same for every reader. The one exception is your unsubscribe link, and all it can tell me is that you want to leave.
- **No mailing-list service.** I keep the list myself, in Sling, encrypted on my Mac. I use your address only for the newsletter and never share or sell it. The emails go out from my own address through Gmail, and your sign-up passes through my Google Workspace on the way.
- **Double opt-in.** I add you to the list only after you confirm through the link in the confirmation email.
- **One-click unsubscribe.** Leaving is easier than joining. Every email has an unsubscribe link, plus the one-click header your mail app uses for its own Unsubscribe button.

All the details, including how long I keep what, are in the [privacy policy](/sites/privacy/#newsletter).
